## Answers to the four questions

**(a) `listen` removal — clean in the design, not in the ADR.**
Every `listen` occurrence in `php-fastcgi.md` is one of: the removal/unknown-key prose (`:190-191`, `:200-202`, `:345`, `:359`, `:382`, `:591`), the generated pool file's INI `listen=`/`listen.mode` (`:1138`, `:1266`, `:1298`, `:1830`), the `listen(2)`/seccomp `SYS_listen` syscall (`:997`, `:1319`, `:1325`, `:1624-1625`), or an explicitly historical alternative/stale-text column (`:1719`, `:1748-1752`, `:2090`, `:2410`, `:2463`). No surviving sentence treats `listen` as a manifest key, a user-authored socket path/mode/parent, or a default source; `workers = 4` survives only in the "goes with them" sentence (`:2051`) and the explicitly-stale §8.2 column (`:2090`). The removal is stated once as a rule (`R1`, `:199-202`) and enforced once as a parser outcome (`UnknownKey`, `:382`, `:1809`, `:1904`). One §9 historical row (`:2462`) still describes a deleted `FastCgiListenParentInvalid` fix without the "Superseded by `MB.3.9.2`" note its neighbours carry (`:2410`, `:2463`) — see C-10.
In the ADR, decision 2's old sentence (`ADR-0008:87-88`, "three keys", "`workers` (default `4`)") survives, but it is immediately annotated with a pointer to `Amends` (`:88-89`); decision 6 also still names the `PoolConf` field `listen` (`:176-177`), which is not reconciled with the design's `socket_path` (C-11).

**(b) Error-variant story — consistent.**
`FastCgiListenInvalid`/`FastCgiListenParentInvalid` are deleted once (`:382`) and the deletion is repeated in the amendment sync and leaf (`:2049-2050`, `:1904`); `listen` is refused as `ManifestError::UnknownKey` in `:382`, `:1809`, `:1904`. `FastCgiWorkersOverBudget` is added and correctly described as a pure `Manifest::check_fastcgi_workers` error, not a `validate` error (`:390-400`, `:1904`); `FastCgiPoolSocketInvalid` is added and limited to the `dev`/`check` probe (`:396-400`, `:1140`, `:1818`). §4.1, §7.2 and §8 use the same names and the same two-number message (`:379`, `:1809`, `:1904`). Defect: the `FastCgiPoolSocketInvalid` example shows a production path although the variant is `dev`/`check`-only (C-07).

**(c) Requirement numbering — coherent, with two stale references.**
`R1`–`R20` exist exactly once, in order (`:199-323`); `R1`, `R3`, `R4` and `R20` are referenced consistently as "§4.1 `R3`"/"`R4`"/"`R20`" (`:29`, `:54-55`, `:272-273`, `:315-318`, `:1809`, `:1818`). Exceptions: `(§R3)` at `:202` is the only malformed cross-reference (C-09); `R3` still describes one probe that "requires the derived leaf to exist" (`:228-230`) while §4.8.1 now has a two-phase parent-before-pool/leaf-after-pool probe (`:1127-1146`) (C-08); and §9 claims both a "`R4` row" (`:2402`) and "there is no `R4` row" (`:2428`) (C-10).

**(d) ADR reads as a documented amendment, but three surviving decisions contradict it.**
It is not a silent edit: the design carries `amended: MB.3.9.2` (`:5`), a dated blockquote (`:23-37`), dated §8.2 sync rows (`:2045-2068`) and explicit "Superseded by `MB.3.9.2`" notes (`:2410`, `:2463`); the ADR carries an inline dated pointer (`:88-89`) and two dated `**Amends.**` bullets (`:272-324`). However, decision 2 still asserts a test that `dev` spawns no `php-fpm` (`:111-115`), decision 1 still says `ensure_ready` "never spawns a pool in `dev`/`check`" (`:48-49`), and decision 6 still says "`dev`/`check` … never spawn pools" plus the now-nonexistent `DevProbe::check` (`:236-237`) — all contradicted by the amendment's own sentence "`blastit dev` spawns and supervises the pool" (`:273`), the normative page (`php.md:34,56`) and `MB.3.9.4`. The `**Amends.**` bullet defers the `dev`/`check` *lifecycle* to `MB.3.9.3` but does not flag that these three decision texts are now false. See C-02, C-03, C-04.
