## Checked and consistent (no finding)

- The page's three `query` refusals are transcribed word for word, with the page's own sites: ``this `query` is empty; write the one statement it grants`` at `9:16`, ``a `query` is one statement; this one starts a second`` at `11:26`, and ```...` is only valid in `IN`/`NOT IN ($?, ...)` and after a `VALUES` row``` at `13:31`. The arithmetic in §13.4.3 (`SELECT 1; ` = 10 decoded chars; `SELECT id FROM ` = 15) is right, and `docs/troubleshooting.md`'s "a `query` at 8 spaces puts its SQL at 16" agrees.
- The two refusals that gain `at` keep their exact words. `error.rs:419-435` and `:439-455` are unchanged in the design's render (`{}{location}: queries[{rule}] …`), `prefix(Position::NONE) == ""` is true (`error.rs:103-109`), and ADR-0003's amendment quotes the same tail. The one permitted protected-file change (`at` on five literals in `critical_manifest_011.rs`) is named in both §18.1 and the brief.
- `pg_query`'s error shape is pinned correctly: `Error::Parse(String)` is filled from `(*result.error).message` (`src/query.rs:35-36`), `cursorpos` exists in the C struct (`libpg_query/pg_query.h:15`, `pg_query_parse.c:101`), and `mod bindings` is private (`src/lib.rs:41`). Only the `error.rs:7` line citation is wrong (C8).
- The grammar's measured counts that do not depend on the stale §9.2 block: 79 case-insensitive keywords; seven `sql_string` children; 23 Blastfiles (`8` fixtures + `6` e2e + `9` python) parse with zero `ERROR`/`MISSING`; `select query(id)` is genuinely an `ERROR` on the committed parser (measured), exactly as §9.3 records.
- The capture set is coherent: §10.1's table and §10.2's block use the same fifteen names, all fifteen are used, `scripts/check_zed_extension.py`'s `CAPTURES` lists the same fifteen, and the amendment adds no name (`sql_number`/`sql_word`/`sql_operator`/`sql_other` stay uncaptured under `(sql_string) @string.special`).
- The ADR amendment dates (`2026-11-18`), nodes (`MB.34.2`), and decisions agree with the design: `codeAction` only, boolean `codeActionProvider`, one `quickfix`, `context.diagnostics` untrusted, `query` leaves `keyword`, precedence above the extras, `sql_string` used only for `'…'`.
- VISION.md's DX-first law is not violated by the amendment's shape: local/CI/deploy remain three short steps, the LSP executes nothing, and the accepted local page's "no `npm`, Tree-sitter CLI, `cargo` or `wasi-sdk`" is preserved. The defects above are coherence defects in the internal contract, not new user steps.

## Verdict

The amendment is close, but C1–C3 are contract-level contradictions a cheap implementer would act on: `Position::NONE` is asked to mean "no document" while two lists send in-document refusals there; the expansion table claims to be the page's and is not; and §1 tells MB.34.4 to return `NONE` for the very two variants this node gives a site. C4–C10 then need a single editing pass over the lists, the `point_at` seam, the grammar record and the corpus names.
