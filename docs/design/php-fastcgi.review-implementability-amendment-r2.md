## VERDICT: FAIL

1 BLOCKER, 7 MAJOR, 9 MINOR.

The round-1 rewrite fixed the *design's* mode set, split the probe into two phases and
made `B2` re-entrant; the normative `B1`/`B2`/mode-table cells are now implementable. But
the companion ADR (and one prose site in the design) still carry the pre-fix `0700`/`0555`
constants, so the document pair does not yet have one directory-mode story. Separately, the
leaf `Lands` cells for `MB.3.5.1`/`MB.3.5.2` lost required rules and required tests while
the design text kept them: a cheap implementer who follows the briefs verbatim ships a
`workers = 0` path that boots and two missing protected oracles. The phase split has no
owning leaf for its phase-2 mapper, and `MB.3.7`'s e2e assertion ("the pool it started
exists") cannot pass at its dependency position because no leaf is told to set
`PoolSpawn::External` for `dev` before `MB.3.9.4`.

## Checked and found unambiguous

- **`B2` is re-entrant and complete.** `mkdirat` both levels with `EEXIST` ok, `fchownat`+`fchmodat`
  the container to `0711`, open `<i>`, then **normalise (`fchownat`+`fchmodat 0700`) before
  `fstat`** (`php-fastcgi.md:1364`). A preserved `<i>` frozen at `0511` by `B3`/`S6` is
  `r-x` to its owner `init`, so the second-boot `openat2` succeeds and the normalise brings it
  back to `0700`; `CAP_CHOWN`/`CAP_FOWNER` are the already-required capabilities. The three
  traversers are each served: `php-fpm` gets `x` from `/`→`fastcgi` (`0755`/`0711`) and `wx`
  on `<i>` only in `S2`; the scheduler gets `x` on `<i>` from `0511` for
  `/proc/self/fd/<parentfd>/fpm.sock`; `init` owns every level.
- **The derived parent cannot be reached from a manifest value.** `check_fastcgi_sockets`
  takes `run_root` + `route_index` and the leaf name is the constant `fpm.sock`
  (`:1177-1180`); `PinnedSocket` validates the name (`BadName` for `/`, `.`, `..`, empty,
  `:694`) and the scheduler connects only through `/proc/self/fd` (`:1023`, `:1261-1265`).
- **`MAX_POOL_WORKERS` is stated once and the omission path is covered** *if* the leaf brief
  is corrected: one constant (`:247`), one formula `workers.unwrap_or(cap)` (`:255`,
  `:1182-1192`), one check called from `stage0` (production and `dev`) and
  `check::execute` (`:415-417`, `:1185-1188`), before both consumers (`:1187-1188`), and
  `resolved > 64` refuses the omitted case (`:250-252`). `Config::validate` forbids a `0`
