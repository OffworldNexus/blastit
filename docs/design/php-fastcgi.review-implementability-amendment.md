# Implementability review — `MB.3.9.2` amendment (2026-11-14)

Lens: **implementability** (cheap-model buildability). Scope: only the 2026-11-14
`MB.3.9.2` amendment in `docs/design/php-fastcgi.md` (banner, §1.1–§1.4, §3 R1/R3/R4/R20,
§4.1, §4.2.1 `RouteState`, §4.5.2, §4.8.1–§4.8.3, §7.2/§7.3, §8 `MB.3.5.1`/`MB.3.5.2`,
§8.1 item 4, §8.2 amended rows) and the two `**Amends.**` bullets of
`docs/decisions/ADR-0008-fastcgi-backend-and-param-lockdown.md`. The pre-amendment
`blastit-manifest` grammar on disk is out of scope by instruction. The accepted decision
(no `listen`, optional `workers`, derived socket, over-budget boot error) is not re-argued.

`docs/decisions/ADR-0008-fastcgi-backend-and-param-lockdown.md` is abbreviated below as
`ADR`.

## VERDICT: FAIL

Two **BLOCKER**s, both in the new mechanics rather than the decision: the derived pool
directory chain is pinned in modes that the `route_uid` process that must `bind()` cannot
traverse, and `DevProbe::check` conflates "create/pin the parent" with "the socket leaf
exists", which makes the `check`/`dev` probe ordering contradictory. A cheap implementer of
`MB.3.5.2` or `MB.3.6.3.1` cannot satisfy both halves of either without inventing an API or
a syscall step the document does not name.
